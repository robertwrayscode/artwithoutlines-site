Get involved

There is room for many hands.

AWL is small and just beginning. The work ahead is real — and so is the welcome. Whether you bring time, expertise, partnership, or curiosity, we'd love to hear from you.

Volunteer

Two ways to be present with kids.

Volunteer · Facilitator

Art Guide

Set up the space, hold steady boundaries, follow the child's lead. Art Guides keep the studio safe and welcoming so creativity can take its own shape.

Volunteer · Support

Creative Companion

Sit with kids who want company. Listen without correcting. Be the steady, kind presence that lets a child trust the room.

Future hires

Roles we'll grow into.

As we incorporate and become operational, we'll begin to bring on part-time staff. We are noting these openings here so future supporters and applicants can see the shape of what we're building.

Part-time · Future

Executive Director

Initially handles operations and finance under board oversight. Grows the organization with care, never speed.

Part-time · Future

Program Director

Designs and facilitates the open studio model. Trains volunteers in compassionate listening and child-led practice.

Part-time · Future

Volunteer & Operations Admin

Coordinates volunteer scheduling, background checks, supplies, and the day-to-day of running events.

Consulting · Future

Grant Writer

Helps us secure foundation funding so programs remain free for every family.

Long-term vision

Where we hope to go.

Once we are established, we are excited to explore partnerships and program models that meet kids where they already are.

  • — 01Houston women's shelters — open studios for children in transition.
  • — 02Texas Children's Hospital — creative respite for patients and siblings.
  • — 03Library and museum pop-ups across the city.
  • — 04A neighborhood community art space, eventually.
  • — 05Schools serving children in vulnerable circumstances.
